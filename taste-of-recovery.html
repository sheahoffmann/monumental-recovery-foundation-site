01 — Why we gather

The people behind the plate.

In every good treatment program there is a kitchen, and in that kitchen is a chef cooking three meals a day for people at the hardest point of their lives. It is skilled, steady, caring work — and it rarely gets a spotlight.

Taste of Recovery is that spotlight. One night a year, chefs from leading treatment programs across the country cook for a room full of the people who know exactly what they do. We celebrate them, we celebrate everyone in this space who shows up for patients daily, and every dollar raised goes toward getting more men into the care they deserve.

03 — The menu

Ten courses. Every plate cooked blind.

Each chef draws a course, from amuse-bouche through dessert, and cooks it for all one hundred guests. Plates arrive with no name attached. Guests taste, score, and only learn who cooked what when the night is over.

04 — Three days

The weekend, course by course.

  1. All day

    Guests and chefs fly in

    Out-of-town guests arrive in Colorado Springs and settle in. Room block details are shared with ticket holders.

  2. Afternoon

    Chefs source and shop

    Competing chefs pick up the ingredients they need for their course.

  3. Evening

    Prep begins

    Chefs get a head start on anything that needs time — stocks, cures, doughs, ferments — ahead of competition night.

  1. Morning

    Golf tournament

    Guests who want to play tee off together, with closest-to-the-pin and longest-drive contests on the course.

  2. Morning

    Chefs in the kitchen

    Meanwhile, all ten chefs are at Recovery Nexus preparing their courses.

  3. Afternoon

    Back to the hotel

    A short break after golf to rest and change into black tie.

  4. Evening

    The gala at Recovery Nexus

    Ten blind courses, live music, a silent auction, and a raffle. Guests vote for their favorite plate, the chefs are revealed, and the winner is presented with the trophy.

  1. All day

    Travel home

    Guests and chefs head home — with a curated gift bag, carry-on friendly, including small-batch jam and hot sauce made by Chef Brian.

05 — The vote

No names. No politics. Just the plate.

  1. A

    Taste blind

    Every course is served without the chef or program attached.

  2. B

    Every guest votes

    At the end of the dinner, each guest casts a ballot for their favorite course.

  3. C

    The reveal

    Chefs are unmasked, the votes are counted, and the winner takes the trophy home.

06 — The trophy

One trophy stays.
One travels.

The permanent trophy lives at Recovery Nexus. Each year the winning chef’s name is engraved on its base, so the plaque grows into a record of the best cooking in our field.

The winner doesn’t leave empty-handed: a traveling trophy goes home with them and stays at their program for the year, until the next champion is crowned.

09 — The point of it all

Every course funds a second chance.

Proceeds go to Monumental Recovery Foundation, which pays for scholarships so men who need extended, high-quality addiction treatment can get it — regardless of what they can afford.

Scholarship dollars are paid directly to approved treatment programs, never as cash, and awards are made on need alone.
