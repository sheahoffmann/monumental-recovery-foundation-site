For applicants and clinicians

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911. Do not wait for a scholarship decision to get help.

Before you apply

Who can apply

  • Men age 18 or older with a substance use disorder
  • Who have been recommended for extended care by a licensed clinician
  • Who cannot cover the full cost of treatment after insurance and family resources

What to send with your application

  • A government-issued photo ID
  • Proof of income: your most recent tax return, 2 recent pay stubs, or a benefits letter
  • Insurance card (front and back) and any approval or denial letter, if insured
  • A program cost estimate or admission letter, if you have one

Copies or clear phone photos are fine.

What happens next

  • Within 2 business days, you will receive an email confirming receipt, your application ID number, and anything that is missing.
  • Once your application is complete, our board of directors reviews it at its next meeting.
  • You will be notified of the decision in writing within 3 business days of the vote.

Your privacy

Your application is confidential. It is reviewed only by the Foundation's board of directors and is identified by an ID number, not your name. We do not share your information with anyone outside the Foundation except the treatment program, and only as you authorize in the application.
