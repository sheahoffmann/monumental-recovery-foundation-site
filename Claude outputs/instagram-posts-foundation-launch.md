## Notes before posting

- **Don't call gifts "tax-deductible."** The foundation's 501(c)(3) status is still pending. If anyone asks, point them to the FAQ on the donate page.
- **Never feature scholarship recipients.** The site promises that no one has to become a fundraising story, and federal privacy rules cover recipient information.
- **Check how the Recovery Nexus link is framed.** The foundation pays approved treatment programs, so Recovery Nexus posts shouldn't suggest scholarships go to Recovery Nexus.
