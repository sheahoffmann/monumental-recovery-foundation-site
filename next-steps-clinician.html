Clinician · Next steps

The Clinical Recommendation is downloading.

Here is what to do next. You can leave this page open while you work.

01

Find the file

It is saved to your computer's Downloads folder as clinical-recommendation.pdf.

Didn't download? Download it again.

02

Fill it out and save it

Open the file and click into each box to type your answers. Write the applicant's full name at the top so we can match it to his application, then complete the assessment and type your name as your signature.

When you're done, save the file (File → Save) so your answers stay in it.

Works best in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader or Preview on a Mac. If you fill it out in a web browser, use the browser's download or save button to keep your answers.

03

Come back and press Submit

Return to the application page at monumentalrecovery.org/apply.html and press Submit. A new email to the Foundation opens. Attach your saved PDF and press Send. It goes directly to the Foundation, not to the applicant.

You can also email it yourself to give@monumentalrecovery.org.

Go to Submit

Questions or trouble with the form? Email give@monumentalrecovery.org.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.
