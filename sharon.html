In loving memory

Sharon Elaine Bunnett

August 7, 1935 – September 15, 2026

In lieu of flowers, her family asks that gifts be made in her memory to Monumental Recovery Foundation, so that men who cannot afford treatment can get it.

Sharon Elaine Bunnett was born on August 7, 1935, and passed on September 15, 2026, at the age of 91. She made her home in Dallas, Texas.

Her family has asked that she be remembered with something lasting rather than with flowers. Her grandson, Will Bunnett, helped build Monumental Recovery Foundation, and gifts given in Sharon's name go toward putting men into treatment they could not otherwise afford.

How to give in her memory

Please send a check.

The family has asked that memorial gifts come by mail rather than online, so every dollar arrives whole and each gift can be acknowledged personally.

Make the check payable to

Monumental Recovery Foundation

Write in the memo line

In memory of Sharon Bunnett

Mail it to

Monumental Recovery Foundation
825 Lakeside Drive
Carbondale, CO 81623

Please include your name and mailing address so the foundation can send you a written acknowledgment, and so the family can be told who gave. If you would like your gift to remain anonymous to the family, just say so in the envelope and we will honor that.

Monumental Recovery Foundation is a Colorado nonprofit corporation. Our application for 501(c)(3) recognition is in process; we will notify donors when our determination letter arrives. Please check with your own tax advisor about deductibility in your situation.

Where these gifts go

Straight to a bed, for a man who is ready.

Memorial gifts join the scholarship fund. Scholarship money is paid directly to an approved treatment program on a man's behalf, never handed out as cash, and every award is documented. Our directors serve without compensation.

If you would like to know more about the work Sharon's memorial gifts are supporting, the story page explains why the foundation exists.

Questions about a memorial gift, or want to arrange something else? Email give@monumentalrecovery.org and a person will answer.

A Colorado nonprofit corporation organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes.
